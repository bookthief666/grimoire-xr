## Product invariant

**ATTUNEMENT REVEALS THE RELIC; IT DOES NOT REWRITE THE RELIC.**

0.40 adds a tactile interaction layer to the focused card face while preserving the canonical semantic boundaries established in 0.32–0.39.

## Interaction contract

- deliberate press-and-hold threshold: 700 ms;
- pointer movement beyond 18 px cancels attunement so swipe navigation remains authoritative;
- successful attunement reveals only correspondence facts already present in the canonical bridge;
- Two of Wands fixture: Dominion / Fire / Mars / Aries;
- interaction glow, timing and reveal animation are `PROJECT_AUTHORED_INTERACTION_CUE_NOT_SOURCE_FACT`;
- SEAL returns the surface to the unrevealed state;
- reduced-motion keeps the semantic reveal but suppresses pulse/progress animation;
- successful reveal may trigger a safe haptic acknowledgement, but haptics never gate correctness;
- attunement does not change card ID, patina, deck ordering, ReadingRecord, source claims, or generated interpretation.

## Pending-source behavior

If a Tarot system does not yet have a reviewed correspondence pack, the surface may reveal the stable relic identity and an explicit source-pack-pending notice. It must not synthesize or infer missing correspondences.
