# 0.42 — Living Book: Oracle Coherence

Base: accepted 0.40.1 Continuity Foundation at `44ed94d936dbe2edba8414490ce0f4238a41a876`.

## Product problem

The completed Oracle reading currently presents a useful canonical ReadingRecord through a developer/QA visual hierarchy: unlabeled cards, one red synthesis box, raw enum-heavy provenance, reason codes, internal source IDs and a contract SHA at normal reading level.

0.42 changes presentation, not semantic truth.

## Product intent

A completed reading should feel like a kept page of a living grimoire:

- the question reads as an epigraph;
- THESIS / ANTITHESIS / SYNTHESIS are visible and explain their role;
- the three cards remain visually dominant;
- generated synthesis is readable, selectable and explicitly marked interpretation;
- deterministic relations are translated into plain scholarly language;
- unsupported relations stay explicitly unsupported;
- the source/method basis is readable without requiring internal enum literacy;
- raw provenance remains available under a collapsed Technical Record;
- the reading can be copied as one coherent text artifact;
- Archive becomes `KEEP THIS READING` at the product surface.

## Authority colors / hierarchy

This slice begins the Living Book grammar without a global redesign:

- brass/gold: canonical method, sources and structural labels;
- bone/ivory: reading prose and user-facing text;
- scarlet: generated interpretation and actions;
- dark ground: negative space;
- emerald/amber/red relation accents are subordinate semantic cues, not global chrome.

No new font dependency is introduced; body reading prose uses a system serif stack.

The global red `GRIMOIRE OS` shell remains unchanged in 0.42. Shell coherence is deliberately deferred to a later cross-route design pass rather than special-casing only the Oracle navbar.

## Semantic invariants

`src/tarotBridge/oracleBookPresentation.js` is a pure presentation adapter. It consumes the existing `reading.readingRecord` and never recomputes Tarot relations.

It must preserve:

- card IDs;
- position IDs;
- orientations;
- relation types;
- reason codes;
- source IDs;
- relation-method authority;
- semantic-contract pin;
- unresolved gaps.

`UNSPECIFIED` is never translated into neutral/friendly/adverse doctrine.

### Reader-facing translations

Examples:

- `FRIENDLY` → `Mutual strengthening` + explicit statement that the pair is classified friendly under the active elemental-dignity method.
- `INIMICAL` → `Contrary relation` + explicit statement that the pair is classified inimical.
- `CARD_WITHOUT_SUIT_FAMILY` → no relation asserted because one or both cards lack a canonical suit family.
- `SOURCE_DOES_NOT_SPECIFY_PAIR` → source remains silent.

These are presentation paraphrases of attached technical facts, not new Tarot facts.

## Progressive disclosure

Normal product view does not lead with:

- `BOUND_TRIAD_CLOTH`;
- `SOURCE_QUALIFIED_METHOD_INHERITANCE`;
- `CARD_WITHOUT_SUIT_FAMILY`;
- raw source IDs;
- contract SHA.

They remain visible under `TECHNICAL RECORD`.

## Fold visual refinement pass

The first Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 6 screenshot confirmed the Living Book direction but exposed several compression issues. Before freeze the surface was refined to:

- use concise position-function wording on Fold/tablet widths while retaining the full canonical question-function on large screens;
- keep long Thoth native titles in the presentation/copy data but hide them from narrow card overlays where they became illegibly small;
- stack relation notes until large-screen widths rather than forcing narrow side-by-side columns;
- enlarge relation, wider-pattern and scholarly-basis typography and line-height;
- turn `COPY READING`, `TECHNICAL RECORD`, `KEEP THIS READING`, and `NEW READING` into deliberate mobile touch targets;
- stack bottom actions on Fold/tablet widths;
- allow the completed reading more horizontal measure without widening the question-entry form.

These refinements change presentation only.
