# 0.48 D2 — Exhaustive Relation Conformance

## Purpose

Prove cross-client behavioral equivalence for the Crowley / Liber LXXVIII relation method before deleting the Fold copy of its relation table or kernel logic.

## Authority boundary

- Contract: `grimoire.tarot.semantic.v1@1.0.0`
- Authoritative repository: `bookthief666/tarot-archetype-vr`
- Authority commit: `f4534b4f92d88f3950ec0c9c211bfa4648cd08ea`
- No new Tarot facts are introduced in this phase.

## Coverage

The matrix uses five relation families: Major/no-suit, Wands (`staffs`), Cups, Swords, and Disks (`coins`). Repeated families use distinct canonical cards so no case depends on impossible duplicate-card draws.

The gate compares:

- 25 ordered family pairs × 2 contexts = **50 pair/context cases**;
- 5 × 5 × 5 = **125 representative three-card family combinations**;
- relation status/type/reason code;
- source IDs and claim IDs;
- dignity-kernel and source-pack versions;
- immediate-neighbor adjacency claims versus outer-pair context;
- outer-pair contextual relation;
- center-between-contraries effect;
- ReadingRecord relation provenance and unresolved reason codes.

## Required explicit invariants

- same-suit Wands strengthens;
- Wands ↔ Cups is inimical;
- Cups ↔ Disks remains `UNSPECIFIED / SOURCE_DOES_NOT_SPECIFY_PAIR`;
- any Major/no-suit pair remains `UNSPECIFIED / CARD_WITHOUT_SUIT_FAMILY`;
- outer-pair facts never inherit the immediate-adjacency claim;
- Wands → Swords → Cups preserves the accepted Three-Aces relation pattern;
- Wands → Major → Cups preserves Major-gap immediate `UNSPECIFIED` while still allowing the source-qualified outer/center context effect.

## Freeze rule

Do not remove or replace the Fold relation table/kernel unless the exhaustive comparator is zero-drift. A failing path is evidence to classify, not a reason to weaken normalization or tests.
