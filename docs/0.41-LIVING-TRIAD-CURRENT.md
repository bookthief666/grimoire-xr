## Product invariant

**THE READINGRECORD IS THE CURRENT. PRESENTATION MAY REVEAL IT; PRESENTATION MAY NOT RECOMPUTE IT.**

0.41 moves the source-safe interaction model from one relic to the relation between three relics.

The Oracle result keeps its existing card row, generated prose, and provenance panel. A new Living Triad Current surface sits between the card row and generated prose and consumes the already-built canonical `reading.readingRecord`.

## Deterministic trace order

A TRIAD trace reveals four ReadingRecord facts in order:

1. Thesis → Antithesis immediate relation;
2. Antithesis → Synthesis immediate relation;
3. Thesis ↔ Synthesis outer-pair context;
4. center-context effect.

After the fourth reveal, `SEAL CURRENT` returns the interaction to zero revealed steps. Sealing changes presentation state only.

## Authority boundary

Trace ordering, borders, glow, button labels, and reveal timing are project-authored UI behavior:

`PROJECT_AUTHORED_RELATION_TRACE_NOT_SOURCE_FACT`

The presentation layer does not import or invoke the canonical relation engine. It consumes fields already present in the ReadingRecord.

## State / persistence boundary

Tracing must not mutate:

- ReadingRecord;
- selected cards or their ordering;
- card identity;
- patina;
- generated Oracle answer;
- source IDs;
- relation method or authority;
- archive payload.

The component owns only an ephemeral reveal count.

## Reduced motion

Reduced-motion mode preserves exactly the same four deterministic facts and trace order. Visual animation may be suppressed without changing semantics.

## Haptics

The real app may emit one uniform light haptic acknowledgement per newly revealed trace step. Haptic intensity is not mapped to `FRIENDLY`, `INIMICAL`, or any other doctrinal meaning.
