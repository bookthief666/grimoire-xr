# 0.45 — Enchanted Threshold & Oracle

Base: `a923dc98060e84e155787a67b21dbf7b46028a5b` (accepted 0.44 Ritual Hybrid + Exalted shell activation)

## Product goal

Make the main ritual loop feel like a magical instrument rather than ordinary content rendered over an occult background.

The preferred visual anchor remains **RITUAL HYBRID + EXALTED**. ARCANE OS and LIVING BOOK remain fully reversible through the 0.44 current system.

## Governing rule

**Magic appears as a consequence of recorded ritual state, never as invented Tarot doctrine.**

Presentation authority:

`PROJECT_AUTHORED_RITUAL_SURFACE_NOT_SOURCE_FACT`

The new visual layer is downstream of existing Threshold state and `buildOracleBookPresentation()` output. It does not read Tarot source tables, build a deck, construct ReadingRecords, calculate dignities, or call providers.

## Threshold

The Threshold becomes a liminal ritual surface:

- large deterministic sixfold seal geometry;
- seven planetary-style ornamental glyphs used as ambient presentation, not correspondence claims;
- three dormant relic silhouettes;
- question inscription increases the field's visual presence;
- `DRAW THREE` enters a short 520 ms ceremonial opening state before invoking the already-existing provider-free draw callback;
- repeated taps are blocked during that opening state;
- reduced motion freezes the new orbital animation.

The delay is presentation-only. It does not alter card selection, the canonical deck, or the ReadingRecord.

## Oracle

The completed Living Book receives a spatial relation field behind the three cards.

The field consumes only the reader-facing relation tone already produced by `oracleBookPresentation.js`:

- `supportive` → warm gold current;
- `contrary` → scarlet current;
- `unresolved` → muted/dashed current;
- outer-context tone → outer arc;
- `centerContext.applied` → center-between-contraries mark.

Unknown tones fail closed to `unresolved`.

No relation is inferred by the visual layer.

### Position seals

The Thesis / Antithesis / Synthesis seals are project-authored position ornament:

- Thesis · triangle;
- Antithesis · diamond;
- Synthesis · circle.

They are not Tarot correspondences and are explicitly covered by the presentation authority boundary.

### Relics

Unmanifested cards gain engraved framing and a stronger ritual-object treatment without manufacturing card artwork or correspondence facts.

### Witness

The deterministic Witness becomes a central illuminated folio with a ritual seal and deeper material hierarchy. The text and authority remain unchanged.

## Reversible aesthetics

The same structure inherits the 0.44 profile system.

- **ARCANE OS**: sharper scarlet/electrical treatment.

Enchantment intensity remains orthogonal. EXALTED is the preferred reference but VEILED/BALANCED/VIVID remain supported.
