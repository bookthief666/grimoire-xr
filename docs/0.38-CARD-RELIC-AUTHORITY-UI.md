## Problem closed

The 2D card modal already used canonical Tarot facts to constrain generation, but it did not expose those facts to the user. The visible card surface mixed:

- the canonical card label;
- generated exegesis;
- generated Hebrew/planet/alchemical/daimon reflection;
- image-generation telemetry;

without making their epistemic authority visually distinct.

That allowed generated reflection to look like historical/source-qualified Tarot fact even though the internal bridge already knew the difference.

## 0.38 invariant

**THE RELIC SHOWS ITS AUTHORITY LAYERS.**

The card modal now separates:

1. **Canonical identity** — stable `cardId` and deck-label authority.
2. **Source-qualified expression** — reviewed Thoth expression fields where available.
3. **Canonical correspondences** — reviewed/derived source-qualified fields where available.
4. **Generated exegesis** — explicitly `MODEL_GENERATED_INTERPRETATION` or legacy-unclassified.
5. **Generated reflection metadata** — explicitly `MODEL_GENERATED_REFLECTION` or legacy-unclassified; never shown as source fact.
6. **Generated visual direction** — explicitly model-generated/legacy-unclassified.
7. **Image generation provenance** — provider/mode/size/steps/seed/denoise where recorded.
8. **Source IDs + semantic contract pin** — visible at the relic level.

For traditions whose expression packs are not source-qualified, the panel states that the visible label is a project compatibility label and claims no source IDs.
