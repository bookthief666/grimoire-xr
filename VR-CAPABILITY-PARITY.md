Image generation remains explicit. A 78-image Grand Forge can take hours on the measured M2 workflow, so the console never starts it from ritual creation and requires an extra arm control for the full run. The batch is resumable in bounded slices during the current session and stops only between provider jobs. Generated bitmaps are exported but deliberately excluded from the lightweight browser palace snapshot.

The remaining XR-specific work is embodiment rather than missing original-app functionality: direct controller/hand dragging on the spatial cloth, positional court audio, teleport and comfort turning, optional encrypted cross-device synchronization, and native OpenXR packaging. Physical Fold 6 and Quest acceptance still determine the final scale and comfort constants.
