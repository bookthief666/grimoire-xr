# Living Invocation 0.11A

This pass closes the gap between the unrestricted original Grimoire interface and the spatial temple. The browser/Fold dossier remains the precision surface for typing any subject, person, deity, text, category, or living question; VR turns that authored current into an embodied instrument instead of replacing it with preset choices.

## What changed

- **No hidden subject preset.** A new or deliberately cleared palace remains blank until the user authors an invocation.
- **Optional voice invocation.** `Speak Invocation` writes recognized speech into the same unrestricted subject field. It is an accelerator only: unsupported browsers keep the full keyboard path.
- **One authoritative current.** Subject, Tarot tradition, aesthetic, Eros, intellect, and Astral Weather remain shared by the dossier, altar, spatial composer, wrist, image forge, and archive.
- **Persistent mnemonic effigy.** When the composer closes, a deterministic holographic manifestation now hovers above the altar. Its geometry, orbit, color, inscription density, pulse, and weather react to the actual authored current and operation state.
- **Bound-deck integrity.** The 78-card architecture is bound to its subject and Tarot lineage. Changing either marks the invocation `UNSEALED` and prevents card, Oracle, Spirit, portrait, or seal operations until the user deliberately rebinds the deck. Aesthetic, Eros, intellect, and weather remain live tunable currents and do not force regeneration.
- **Honest review behavior.** Reopening an unchanged current offers a return to the temple rather than implying that all 78 cards must be regenerated.
- **Safe portable archives.** If an unsealed draft exists, export names and describes the preserved bound palace and carries the draft separately instead of combining a new subject with an old deck.
- **Wrist legibility.** The Wrist Grimoire shows the subject, tradition, style, Eros, intellect, and `BOUND`/`UNSEALED` state at a glance.

## Test the pass

1. Open `/vr` with a fresh browser profile or clear only the Grimoire palace state. Confirm the subject is blank.
2. Type an unrestricted phrase, or choose `Speak Invocation` and dictate one. Select Tradition, Aesthetic, Eros, Intellect, and Astral Weather in the same dossier.
3. Open the spatial composer. Confirm its seven direct-access sigil tabs reflect the dossier, and the holographic effigy changes as style/Eros/intellect/weather change.
4. Awaken the 78 arcana. Close the composer and confirm the effigy remains above the altar, now active and operation-reactive.
5. Reopen the unchanged dossier. Confirm the final action reads `Seal Bound · Return to Temple` instead of asking to regenerate.
6. Change only the aesthetic, Eros, intellect, or weather. Confirm the deck remains usable and the effigy changes.
7. Change the subject or Tradition. Confirm the altar, command deck, and wrist clearly say the invocation is unsealed; generation tools must route back to rebind.
8. Export while unsealed. Confirm the archive filename and top-level subject still identify the preserved palace, while JSON contains a separate `draftInvocation` object.
9. Rebind, then scribe, manifest, cast an Oracle spread, speak with Spirit, and export the archive.

## Deliberate boundaries

- Browser speech recognition varies by platform and may be absent on Quest. Keyboard entry remains authoritative and always available through the ritual console.
- The effigy is deterministic geometry, not a claim to reconstruct a literal person. A future image/depth pipeline may add an optional generated holographic portrait after the interaction and performance contract is proven.
- Physical Quest acceptance is still required for controller-ray scale, wrist placement, text comfort, and measured 72 Hz performance.
